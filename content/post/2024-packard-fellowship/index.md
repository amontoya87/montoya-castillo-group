Andrés has been named a 2024 Packard Fellow for Science and Engineering by
the David and Lucile Packard Foundation. The
[Packard Fellowships](https://www.packard.org/insights/news/the-david-and-lucile-packard-foundation-announces-the-2024-class-of-packard-fellows-for-science-and-engineering/)
are among the largest nongovernmental awards for early-career scientists
and engineers in the United States, providing flexible, multi-year support
for the most innovative researchers.
