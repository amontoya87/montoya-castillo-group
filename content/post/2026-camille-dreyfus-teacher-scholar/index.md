Andrés has been named a [2026 Camille Dreyfus
Teacher-Scholar](https://www.dreyfus.org/2026-camille-dreyfus-teacher-scholar-awards/)
by the Camille and Henry Dreyfus Foundation. The Camille Dreyfus
Teacher-Scholar Awards recognize accomplished early-career faculty in the
chemical sciences whose research achievements and dedication to education
mark them as future leaders of the field.
