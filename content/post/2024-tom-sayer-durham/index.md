Congratulations to **Tom Sayer** on winning a Leverhulme Early Career
Fellowship to support an independent postdoctoral position at Durham
University. Tom joined the group as a postdoctoral researcher in 2021 and
has been a driving force behind much of our work on generalized quantum
master equations and data-driven climate dynamics.

We're excited to follow what comes next — and to keep collaborating.
