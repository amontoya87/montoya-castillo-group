Congratulations to Anthony for his paper "[A stochastic cluster expansion for electronic correlation in large systems](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c00563)," just published in *The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters*! Together with Vojtěch Vlček's group and Libor Veis, the paper introduces a method that recovers near-DMRG-accurate correlation energies for large systems without choosing an active space in advance, opening the door to high-accuracy studies of chemistry in condensed-phase environments.
