Congratulations to Tom for the Perspective "[Hiding in plain sight: The prevalence and impact of trions and Fermi polarons in transient absorption spectroscopy experiments of 2D semiconductors](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/161/19/190901/3320834)," just published in *The Journal of Chemical Physics*, and extra congratulations on its selection as a Featured Article and the journal cover! Written with the Sambur and Krummel groups, the Perspective offers a unifying explanation of how trions and Fermi polarons shape transient absorption spectra of 2D semiconductors, and shows how different interpretations of the same data lead to different timescales and mechanisms.
