Congratulations to Srijan and Tom for their paper "[Mori generalized master equations offer an efficient route to predict and interpret polaron transport](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/sc/d4sc03144j)," just published in *Chemical Science*! The work introduces a tool that minimizes the cost of predicting conductivities in small-polaron-forming materials, and a way to map measured conductivities onto polaron energy scales, overcoming the limitations of the phenomenological Drude–Smith model.
