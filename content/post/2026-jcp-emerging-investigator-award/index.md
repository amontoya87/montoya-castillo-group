Andrés has received the 2025 *Journal of Chemical Physics* Best Theoretical
Paper by an Emerging Investigator Award, which recognizes outstanding
theoretical work published in the journal by an early-career researcher.

The award recognizes the paper
"[Space-local memory in generalized master equations: Reaching the
thermodynamic limit for the cost of a small lattice
simulation](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/162/9/091102/3338405/)" by
Srijan Bhattacharyya, Thomas Sayer, and Andrés, *J. Chem. Phys.* 162, 091102
(2025). See the [JCP awards page](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/pages/awards)
for details.
