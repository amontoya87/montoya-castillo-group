Congratulations to Anthony for his paper "[A pedagogical tour of the Fourier transform with applications to NMR and IR spectroscopy](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acs.jchemed.4c01439)," just published in the *Journal of Chemical Education*! With Nick Cipolla, William Pfalzgraff, Jeffrey Jankowski, and Becky Rapf, Anthony created three self-contained, Python-based lab activities that teach undergraduates the Fourier transform by analyzing audio signals, an IR interferogram, and an NMR free induction decay.
