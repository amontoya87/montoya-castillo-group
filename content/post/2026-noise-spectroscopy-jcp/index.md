Nanako Shitara, with Andrés, has published [Fast, accurate, and
error-resilient noise spectroscopy via basis
optimization](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/164/6/061101/3379373) in
*The Journal of Chemical Physics* (vol. 164, 061101, 2026).

The work introduces an adaptive approach to quantum noise spectroscopy that
delivers higher accuracy with shorter experimental runs — an important step
toward making quantum sensors practical in the presence of realistic noise.
